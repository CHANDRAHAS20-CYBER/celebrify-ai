Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I need an account?

No. Your draft is saved automatically in this browser as you work, right up until you download it.

Where do my photos go?

Nowhere but your own browser and, if you download it, your own file. Photos are embedded directly into the file you save.

What formats can I download?

A standalone HTML page (works offline, keeps any photos embedded), a PNG image, and a print-ready version you can save as a PDF.

Is it really free to try?

Designing the card and previewing it live is free. A small one-time payment unlocks the actual downloads.
